    How a backtest result is computed

    Every number on this page comes from the same five steps. The engine is backtest/orb/engine.py; it doesn't read the Pine scripts, it re-implements their rules.

    Price dataDatabento CME 1-minute bars, Oct 2021 → Oct 2026. MNQ.v.0 for MNQ; ES.v.0 prices for MES (same price, $5/pt).
    Simulate each dayBuild the opening range, wait for a breakout, set stop and target, size the position, then walk the 1-minute bars to the exit (target, stop, or end of day).
    Trade listOne row per trade: entry, stop, exit, contracts, $ P&L, and R = P&L ÷ the $ that was at risk.
    Daily P&LEvery weekday counts, including days with no trade (P&L = 0). That's what Sharpe and drawdown are computed from.
    MetricsTotal R, win %, profit factor, Sharpe, max drawdown. Then the same for the in-sample and out-of-sample halves separately.

    The rules the simulator applies each day

    1. Opening range: the highest high and lowest low of the 1m bars from the range start for N minutes (e.g. 09:30–10:00 New York).
    2. Breakout: close = the first signal bar (1m or 5m) that closes beyond the range, filled at that close; touch = a stop order one tick beyond the range, filled when price trades there. One trade a day at most.
    3. Stop: the opposite side of the range (or its midpoint). Target: entry ± RR × (entry − stop).
    4. Size: contracts = floor($500 ÷ (stop distance × $/pt + $1.24)), capped at 10. If that's 0, the day is skipped.
    5. Exit: check every 1m bar after entry. If a bar touches both stop and target, the stop wins (conservative). Still open at the flat time → exit at that bar's open ("EOD").
    6. Costs: $1.24 per contract round trip, plus 1 tick of slippage on the entry, the stop and the EOD exit (none on the target, a limit order).

    How we know whether a result is real

    Testing many settings and keeping the best one finds luck as well as edge. Three checks separate the two.

    2. Walk-forward (picked blind)

    Every 6 months, pick the best setting using only the previous 24 months, then trade it for the next 6 months. Only the colored test windows count. This is the closest thing to "what would have happened if I'd used this method live".

    Glossary

    R
    The money a trade planned to risk: (entry − stop) × $/pt × contracts + commission. A trade that hits a 1R target makes about +1R; a full stop-out is about −1R. Total R lets you compare strategies regardless of position size. Here 1R ≈ $500 (1% of $50k), less when the contract cap or rounding binds.
    Win %
    Share of trades with positive P&L.
    Profit factor (PF)
    Gross wins ÷ gross losses. 1.0 = break even; 1.2 = $1.20 won per $1 lost.
    Sharpe
    Average daily return ÷ its standard deviation, × √252 (annualised). Uses every weekday. Above 1 is good for a single strategy; below 0.5 is weak.
    Max drawdown
    The largest fall from a running peak of the equity curve, in R or $.
    In-sample (IS)
    The first 70% of days (to about Apr 2025). Results here helped choose the setting, so they flatter it.
    Out-of-sample (OOS)
    The last 30% of days. Still part of the five years I looked at when ranking, but a setting that only worked in-sample shows up here.
    Robust score
    min(IS Sharpe, OOS Sharpe). The ranking rule on this page.
    Walk-forward
    Repeatedly choose on a past window and trade the next window blind. The most honest estimate of live performance.
    Touch vs close entry
    Touch: a resting stop order one tick past the range; fills as soon as price gets there. Close: wait for a 1m or 5m bar to close past the range, then enter at market.
    EOD
    End of day: still open at the flat time, closed at market.

    Caveats

    • About 2,400 settings were tested on one five-year sample. Even the robust score uses data I looked at, so expect live results below these numbers; the walk-forward totals are the realistic guide.
    • The engine checks exits on 1m bars and fills EOD at the open, so results won't exactly match the TradingView strategy tester.
    • MES results use ES prices with MES contract terms ($5/pt).
